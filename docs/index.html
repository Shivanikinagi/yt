Short, original explainers about AI, technology and computer science.

What this is

TechDecoded is a YouTube channel that publishes daily Shorts explaining new AI models, research papers, programming concepts and surprising technology facts. Every video links to the sources it is based on.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@techdecoded60-y6b

About the TechDecoded uploader app

The channel owner uses a private tool, the TechDecoded uploader, to produce and publish these videos. The tool researches public technology news feeds, writes an original script, generates narration with text-to-speech and animated graphics, and uploads the finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.

The tool signs in with Google only to upload videos to the channel owner's account. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's Google or YouTube data. See the privacy policy for details.

AI disclosure

Scripts are written with the help of AI language models, narration is synthetic speech, and visuals are computer-generated. Videos are marked as containing altered or synthetic content on YouTube.
