Privacy policy

TechDecoded uploader · Last updated 12 October 2026

This policy explains how the TechDecoded uploader app ("the app") handles data. The app is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the TechDecoded YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner, on that owner's own channel.

Google user data the app accesses

When the channel owner authorizes the app, it requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the owner's own channel name and the status of videos it uploaded, for example whether processing has finished.

The app does not access the YouTube or Google data of any other person. It does not read viewer information, comments, subscribers, email, contacts or files.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to upload, and check the status of, the channel owner's own videos. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI or machine learning models.

Storage and security

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in the owner's private storage: a git-ignored folder on the owner's computer and encrypted GitHub Actions secrets in the owner's private repository. Records of uploaded videos (video ID, title, upload time and publish status) are kept in the owner's private repository.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. Videos are sent only to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API. Script text is generated by a third-party AI model from public news articles, and no Google user data is sent to that service.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can no longer reach the account. On request, the stored tokens and upload records are deleted.
